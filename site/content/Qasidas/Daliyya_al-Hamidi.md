## About

### Notes on the text

From the editor's notes in the source PDF:

- Verse 16: *arid* (أَرِدِ) is from *wurūd*, coming to water, not from *irāda*.
- Verse 18: *turid* (تُرِدِ) is from *irāda*. One manuscript reads:
  إِنْ كُنْتَ أَنْتَ لِهَذَا الأَمْرِ لَمْ تُرِدِ
- Verse 20: *yaḥid* (يَحِدِ) is from *ḥāda yaḥīdu*, to swerve. Printed copies reading
  *yajid* are mistaken and change the meaning.
