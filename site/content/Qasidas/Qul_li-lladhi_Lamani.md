## About

A poem of divine love addressed to the one who blames the lover, opening with the tahlil.
It ends by saying that whoever draws near and tastes the secret of annihilation will speak
as the lover has spoken, and is likewise excused.

This page has part of the qasida, not the full poem.

### Author

Shaykh Muḥammad al-Ḥabīb Hamū al-Būzīdī.
