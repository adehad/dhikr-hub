## About

A plea to the descendants of the Prophet ﷺ for mercy and union, made through Abū Bakr, ʿUmar,
ʿAlī and ʿUthmān. It praises the rank of the family of al-Zahrāʾ, asks for the wine of love,
and ends with blessings on the Prophet ﷺ, his family, his Companions and those who followed them.

Sung in maqām Bayātī. Melody by ʿAbd al-Wahhāb Shamālī al-Ḥalabī.
