## About

A poem on patience in hardship. It promises relief to the patient, compares misfortunes to
clouds that soon clear, and urges trust in Allah, contentment, gratitude in every state, and
following the Prophet Muhammad.

### Author

Imām ʿAbdullāh b. ʿAlawī al-Ḥaddād.
