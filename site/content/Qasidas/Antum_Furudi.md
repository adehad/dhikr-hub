## About

A poem of divine love in which the Beloved is the lover's prayer, qibla and constant concern.
It retells the story of Moses seeing the fire, being spoken to and the mountain crumbling as a
description of the lover's own experience, so that he becomes "the Moses of his time".

The refrain (lāzima) of tahlil and blessings on the Prophet ﷺ is the one printed with the poem
in the source; the source notes that a lāzima may not be part of the original poem.

### Author

ʿUmar ibn al-Fāriḍ.
