## About

A poem of advice to the brothers of the path. It counsels keeping the company of the good
and of a shaykh, holding to the dhikr given with permission, weighing spiritual experiences
against the Shariʿa, and lists the stations of certainty, ending with blessings on the
Prophet ﷺ, his family and Companions.

### Author

Shaykh Muḥammad ibn al-Ḥabīb.
